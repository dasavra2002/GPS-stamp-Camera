## GeoStamp Camera v1.5.0 — A redesigned camera that shoots faster

This release rebuilds the camera screen around a stock-camera layout, makes the shutter much quicker, and finishes restyling the remaining screens.

### New camera layout
- The camera screen stays in portrait. When you turn the phone sideways, the icons and labels rotate to read upright instead of the whole layout reflowing, which fixes clipped pills and the mode label hiding under the status bar.
- Top bar: flash, timer, grid and a quick-settings button, with settings on the right.
- Under the viewfinder: pro controls, a lens zoom pill (`0.6 · 1x · 2`, or zoom presets on single-lens phones) and move stamp. They sit off the preview so they never cover the stamp.
- Pull up the handle for the tools sheet: Night, Portrait, Punch in, PiP, Document, Voice note, Scan QR, Read sign, AI caption, Ghost and Gallery.
- A swipeable mode strip, then thumbnail, shutter and flip.
- Quick settings: HDR, RAW, ratio, timer, burst (or interval / slow-mo speed in those modes), grid, level and settings.
- The GPS pill now sits on the viewfinder, and the histogram stays hidden until it has data.
- The stamp preview is shown at the true size and position it will have on the saved photo, including in landscape. The level indicator turns with the phone.

### Faster capture
- Photos use the camera's low-latency capture mode (Night, Portrait and HDR keep maximum quality).
- The shutter is ready again as soon as the camera hands back the frame. The viewfinder blinks and a thumbnail appears right away.
- Stamping, signing, EXIF and saving happen in the background, one at a time, and finish even if you leave the screen. Up to 3 shots can queue; a ring on the thumbnail shows saves in progress.
- The stamp's text, place, time and number are taken at the moment you press the shutter.
- The share button next to the thumbnail is gone. Long-press the thumbnail to share.

### UI polish
- Edit details, voice memo, Bluetooth GPS, pro controls and the signature dialog now match the rest of the app: section cards with icons, theme colours instead of hardcoded ones, and icons instead of emoji.
- The signature dialog and PDF report no longer claim a "SHA-256 HMAC" seal the report never applied.

### Under the hood
- The camera screen is split into a ViewModel and smaller components, which makes it easier to maintain.
- CI checks the signing secrets and shows which certificate signed each APK.

### Install
Download **GeoStamp-Camera-v1.5.0.apk** below (Android 8.0+ / API 26 to Android 16 / API 36).

If Android refuses to install it over an older version with a "package conflicts" or signature error, uninstall the old version first. Photos you already saved stay in your gallery.
